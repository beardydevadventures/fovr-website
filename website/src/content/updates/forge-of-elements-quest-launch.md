*From the release archive.*

Forge of Elements arrived on Meta Quest on 21 March 2025. The idea was simple: take elements like fire, water, air and earth, put two together, and see what happens.

Each discovery gives you another combination to try. The world changes as you craft, turning a blank landscape into a place of your own making.
