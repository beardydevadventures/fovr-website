*From the release archive.*

Parcel Pummel arrived on Meta Quest on 5 August 2025. The conveyors are down, the parcels are piling up, and it’s your job to get them into the right delivery trucks.

Match the colours, grab a parcel and throw it before time runs out. The trick is keeping your aim when the warehouse starts getting busy.
